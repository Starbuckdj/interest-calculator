JUDGMENT INTEREST CALCULATOR

Privacy Policy

Effective July 10, 2026

Judgment Interest Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

The short version: There are no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, and no network connection. The app runs entirely offline on your iPhone. We never see your numbers, your dates, or your matters.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, or any other personal data. The figures you type — principal amounts, interest rates, dates, and payments — are used only to perform the calculation on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

How your data is stored

The values you enter live only in the app's memory while you use it, on your device. The app has no server component and maintains no database of users or usage. We have no ability to access anything you enter.

Network access and third parties

The app works fully offline and makes no network requests. It contains no third-party advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no social-media integrations. Because nothing leaves your device, there is no data for any third party to receive.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use analytics, cookies, advertising identifiers, or any form of tracking, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children's privacy

The app is a professional calculation tool and is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised. Any future version that collects data would be described here before that version ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to admin@dk4gtech.com.

Legal notice

Not legal advice. Using this app does not create an attorney-client relationship in any way and does not constitute legal advice. Consult the applicable state and local laws regarding pre- and post-judgment interest rates.
