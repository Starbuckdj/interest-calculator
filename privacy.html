JUDGMENT INTEREST CALCULATOR

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · iPhone and Android

Judgment Interest Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

The short version: There are no accounts, no analytics, and no trackers. Calculations run on your iPhone or Android device. The app may download a public rate file. It does not upload your numbers, your dates, or your matters. Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, or any other personal data. The figures you type — principal amounts, interest rates, dates, and payments — are used only to perform the calculation on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

How your data is stored

The values you enter live only in the app's memory while you use it, on your device. The app has no server component and maintains no database of users or usage. We have no ability to access anything you enter.

Network access and third parties

The app may download one public rate file, rates.json, so a later federal week or variable state rate can be added without an app update. That request sends no judgment amount, date, or other information you type. If the file cannot be reached, or a figure in it is missing or out of range, the app uses the rates shipped with it. A copy of the last valid file is kept on your device. Like any web request, the server hosting the file (GitHub Pages) receives your device's IP address; DK4G does not receive or log it. The app contains no third-party advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no social-media integrations.

Purchases

State packs are one-time in-app purchases. On iPhone they are processed by Apple through the App Store; on Android they are processed by Google through Google Play Billing. Apple or Google handles your payment details under its own privacy policy, and we never receive them. On Android the app asks Google Play which packs you own each time it opens and does not store that list itself. Purchases do not carry over between iPhone and Android.

Android app

  • Amounts, dates, payments and results stay in memory and reset when the app process ends.
  • Settings kept on the device: your selected jurisdiction, review-prompt counters, a SHA-256 fingerprint used to avoid counting the same calculation twice (not a saved report), and the last valid copy of the rate file.
  • Android backup is turned off for the app. To remove what it keeps, open Android Settings > Apps > Judgment Interest > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Network use is limited to Google Play Billing and the rate file described above.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use analytics, cookies, advertising identifiers, or any form of tracking, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children's privacy

The app is a professional calculation tool and is not directed to children under 13. Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised. Any future version that collects data would be described here before that version ships.
